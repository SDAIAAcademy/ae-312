# AI Cost Engineering and Model Routing

هندسة تكلفة الذكاء الاصطناعي وتوجيه النماذج

AI Engineering Track · SDAIA Academy · AE-312 · Level 300

Make the cost of language model applications measurable, attributable and smaller, without losing quality

Participants make the cost of language model applications measurable, attributable and smaller without losing quality. For a fictional telecom operator running three applications behind one gateway, a bilingual care assistant, a call-summary job and a billing-dispute agent, they work from a month of synthetic usage traces: they model cost per request and session, attribute spend to teams, set budgets, evaluate model tiers and reasoning settings on their own tasks, route with fallbacks, measure prefix and semantic caches, compare hosted and self-hosted serving and report cost per successful outcome. Cluster capacity planning is AE-307; engine-level optimisation is AE-401.

**Format:** Intensive: 3 teaching days of four sessions and a capstone day, with guided pre-work and a tracked follow-up task

**Course page:** https://sdaiaacademy.github.io/ae-312/

## Case study

**Mawj Telecom (موج للاتصالات)** is a fictional mobile and fibre operator. Three applications share one model gateway, Maabar: **Anis**, a bilingual customer-care assistant; **Khulasa**, a job that summarises calls; and **Fasil**, an agent that handles billing disputes. Every lab works on one month of their synthetic data: a usage log of 97,318 requests, a provider invoice of SAR 8,242.62, the hours of a leased accelerator node, gateway keys and teams, 100 labelled tasks per application in Arabic and English with recorded outputs for three model tiers, replayed traffic for the caches, a throughput benchmark, a classification register and the reviewed outcomes of the disputes.

All of it is generated by [`labs/prepare_data.py`](labs/prepare_data.py) with a fixed seed. The prices are a synthetic sheet in SAR per million tokens, shaped like published prices and equal to none. There is no real customer, no real personal data and no real provider in the data.

## Capstone

A cost review of an unseen scenario: a public repository with the notebook, measured routing and caching results and a two-page memo with three savings options, defended in ten minutes

**Submission:** a public GitHub repository link. The repository holds the notebook with its execution log, the gateway log of the replayed spike, the router file, the cache measurements with the isolation test, and the two-page review. The review has three savings options, each with its measured saving, its effect on quality in Arabic and English, its risk, and energy per thousand requests beside cost. In the ten-minute live defence the participant recomputes one savings figure the assessor picks and explains one routing decision the assessor selects.

The scenario is another month at Mawj, generated with other volumes and another seed by [`labs/capstone/make_scenario.py`](labs/capstone/make_scenario.py). The brief and the rubric are in [`labs/capstone/README.md`](labs/capstone/README.md).

## Assessment and AI policy

| Part | Share |
|---|---|
| Supervised check without AI assistance, on cost arithmetic and caching trade-offs | 20% |
| Capstone repository, on the five-criterion rubric | 60% |
| Live defence of the review | 20% |

A participant passes with 70% overall and at least 60% on the capstone rubric.

AI assistance is off for the supervised check and for the first cost model (Lab 1.1). In the other labs an assistant may explain a query or a configuration and does not produce the analysis. In the capstone AI assistance is allowed in full, and the participant defends the work live without it.

The practice questions in [`assessments/bank.json`](assessments/bank.json) are public and are for self-study. The supervised check is not in this repository.

## Before and after the course

**Guided pre-work.** Before Day 1: run [`labs/00_setup_check.ipynb`](labs/00_setup_check.ipynb) on a laptop or in Colab; read the case study section of [`labs/README.md`](labs/README.md) and open the invoice and the key register of Mawj Telecom; and price one request of your own application by hand from its provider's price page, with input, cached and output tokens kept apart.

**Tracked follow-up task.** Within a month of the capstone, each participant brings one saving from their own organisation's usage that reached an invoice, with its quality figure beside it, and the trainer records it.
