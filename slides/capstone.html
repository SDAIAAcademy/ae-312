Walk through each criterion with one example of full marks, from labs/capstone/README.md. Cost model: within 2% of the invoice, with cached tokens, reasoning tokens, billed timeouts and the node each accounted for. Routing: a table per language with counts, and what the check misses. Caching: a wrong-answer rate and an isolation test that passes. Hosting: the register read before any price. Review: a total the invoice can show, an owner per action, one refusal. The repository counts for 60% of the result and needs 60% on this rubric; the defence counts for 20%.

Format

10 minutes for each defence

1 · The month · 2 minutes

Three numbers and the decision you ask for. Your review is on the screen.

2 · One figure, live · 5 minutes

The assessor picks a savings figure. You recompute it from the log and say what it includes and what it leaves out.

3 · One routing decision · 3 minutes

The assessor selects it. Which requests go where, what the check misses, what would change your mind.

No assistant during the defence. Peer feedback after each one: a strength, a question and a suggestion.

The defence is where authorship shows: AI assistance is allowed for the repository and the review, and not here.
